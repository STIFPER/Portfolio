Client

Wangwela Spa

Year

2024

Services

Branding, Logo

Role

Lead Designer

The Challenge

Wangwela required a brand identity that bridged the gap between traditional Thai healing arts and modern wellness aesthetics. The goal was to create a visual language that felt organic, calming, and premium without being clichés.

The Solution

We developed a logo symbol inspired by the "Hand" (touch) and "Circular Flow" (time/cycle), representing the restoration of balance. The color palette uses deep earth tones mixed with soft creams to evoke a sense of grounding and peace.
